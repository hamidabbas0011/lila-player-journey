# Walkthrough recording checklist

Record a concise 2–4 minute screen capture after importing the data and validating the map projection.

1. Open the dashboard and show the imported event count.
2. Switch between Ambrose Valley, Grand Rift and Lockdown.
3. Filter by date and then by a single match.
4. Toggle human players, bots and event markers.
5. Show player journey paths and the distinct kill, death, storm and loot markers.
6. Switch through kill-zone, death-zone and traffic heatmap modes.
7. Use the timeline playback for a single selected match.
8. Present three evidence-backed findings from INSIGHTS.md.

Only demonstrate features that have been validated against the real dataset.
